Alejandro Mahillo Cazorla — Department of Mathematics, Statistics and Computation, Universidad de Cantabria, Santander, Spain.

Presented at the [XVIII Congress GAFEVOL 2026](https://sites.google.com/view/gafevol-2026/schedule), Universidad de Talca, Talca, Chile.

## Abstract

Semigroup characterizations of function spaces provide a useful way of measuring regularity through the behaviour of solutions to evolution equations. In the continuous setting, classical results of Taibleson and Stein characterize Besov and Hölder spaces in terms of the heat and Poisson semigroups. More recently, Dai, Saksman, Yang, Yuan and Zhang obtained analogous characterizations using fractional heat semigroups.

The aim of this talk is to present recent results in the discrete setting, where we characterize discrete Besov spaces through fractional discrete heat semigroups, with the Poisson semigroup as a particular case. We will discuss estimates for the associated fractional kernels and compare these spaces with those defined through the discrete heat semigroup. If time permits, we will present applications to non-local discrete operators and endpoint maximal regularity.
